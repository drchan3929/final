About Everyday Art

Art is not just found in museums and galleries. It is all around us, in our neighborhoods, public spaces, schools, and everyday experiences.

Our Purpose

Everyday Art helps people discover inspiring artwork and learn about artists who may not yet be widely known. We want to make art more accessible and encourage people to look at their surroundings in new ways.

Our Community

We believe art can bring people together. By sharing artwork, exploring different creative styles, and learning about artists' experiences, visitors can find inspiration and connect with others who appreciate creativity.

Our Vision

Our goal is to create a welcoming space where artists can share their work and everyone can discover something new, regardless of their background or experience with art.
